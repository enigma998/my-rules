# **About Us**
We are a duo of Game Masters experienced in running text-based roleplays. I know Avrae and other Dice Bots can seem daunting and complicated, but we are both here to help you and get you used to using what it offers.
# **About Enigma**
I'm Enigmatic. I have a long history of freeform play by post games. I've only recently gotten into tabletop and I'm addicted to it. I particularly love to DM games, and I like all pillars of play.
I use he/him pronouns.

One of us will likely be participating in the game and teaching players the play by post format and the Avrae VTT.
# **General Rules**
- Be respectful to each other and to the Game Master(s).
- These games may use Avrae and Discord (or another platform). They are not done by voice. If you don't understand Avrae, I have useful links below. The best way to learn is to come and play!
- Nothing overly graphic/sexual/mature in a public setting unless otherwise specified.
- Some swearing is acceptable, just keep it to a minimum.
- If you'd like to do something and you're not sure if it's acceptable for the playgroup, ask me.
- When confused about a ruling, please keep the out-of-character chat to a minimum.
- Don't metagame. If you've played the adventure before, do what your character would do and let others who haven't played it take charges.
- There will be skill overlap between characters, trust your DM to allow every character to shine. Do not bully or gaslight other players into changing their characters.
- Have fun! If this isn't your cup of tea, let me know. Rather than sticking through something you wouldn't enjoy.
# **Attendance Policies**
- Play-by-Post games are a subscription basis.  If you go quiet for longer than three days without word, I will inquire to make sure you're okay.
- Please let us know if you need a break from the game.  We will accommodate -- either by me including side-quests for those present, or us slowing down for the duration.  Or setting you to free while you wait.
- If you have to take a leave (IE, on vacation, etc), I will drop your charge for that week.
- If 2+ players have to take a week off, we'll discuss what we will do (some side RP, or taking a break).  I have plenty of one-shots I can incorporate into the games.
- If I am going to be very slow due to vacation, sickness, etc, you will not be charged the week's fee.  The game will still be updated at that time, but slowly.  I will let you know about these planned vacations.  **There are currently no planned holidays. I run games during the holidays (and sometimes Dreams fills in for Eni).**
